How to Automatically Turn WhatsApp College Notices into Phone Calendar Alarms

By Suraj Gore • October 13, 2026 • 4 min read

If you are an engineering student in India, your college WhatsApp groups are probably flooded with hundreds of messages every single day. Between committee notices, faculty updates, hackathon flyers, and CR announcements, crucial registration cutoffs get buried.

The Problem with Manual Copy-Pasting

Most students take screenshots of flyers or copy text into note apps. But note apps do not ring alarms. When the deadline arrives, the notice is forgotten. Manually typing title, date, time, and link into Google Calendar takes over two minutes per event — so students rarely do it.

Step 1: Install Vanko from Google Play

Download Vanko for free from the Google Play Store. It is lightweight, ad-free, and designed specifically for Indian college and university campuses.

Step 2: Share Notice Directly from WhatsApp

When an event announcement arrives in your WhatsApp group:

  1. Long-press the message to select the text.
  2. Tap the Share button in WhatsApp.
  3. Select Vanko from your Android Share Sheet.

Step 3: AI Instant Extraction & Local Alarms

In approximately 400 milliseconds, Vanko's high-speed AI parser identifies:

  • Event Title & Type: (Hackathon, Workshop, Deadline, Seminar)
  • Dates: Start Date, End Date, and Registration Cutoff
  • Venue / Mode: Auditorium, Labs, or Online Link
  • Automatic Reminders: Exact notifications scheduled for 3 days prior, 24 hours prior, and on the day of the event.
