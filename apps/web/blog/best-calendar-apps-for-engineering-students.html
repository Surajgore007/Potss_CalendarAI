Top 5 Calendar & Productivity Apps for Engineering Students in India (2026)

By Suraj Gore • October 13, 2026 • 6 min read

Managing an engineering degree in India is intense: practical submissions, internal assessments, university exams, and weekend hackathons arrive all at once. Standard calendars designed for corporate offices often fail to meet student needs.

1. Vanko: Best for Indian Campus Life & WhatsApp Notices

Vanko was built specifically for students. Instead of manually creating calendar entries, you simply share or paste unstructured WhatsApp notices into the app. In under a second, AI parses dates, deadlines, and links, scheduling automatic alarms. Additionally, its campus community hub lets students see verified campus events and classmate attendance in real time with 100% offline-first reliability.

2. Google Calendar: Best for Basic Meeting Sync

Google Calendar is reliable for Google Meet links and corporate schedules, but lacks automatic message parsing and student community event discovery.

3. Notion: Best for Notes and Project Wikis

Notion is great for tracking course syllabi, but its mobile app can be heavy, requires constant internet access, and lacks reliable native Android alarm notifications.

4. Microsoft To-Do: Best for Simple Task Checklists

Good for day-to-day checklists, but does not provide calendar view integration or campus group broadcasts.
