Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 • Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 Compliant

1. Overview

Vanko ("we", "our", or "the app") provides a smart campus calendar and event extraction utility for students. We believe privacy is a fundamental human right. We do not sell your personal data, we do not run third-party advertising trackers, and our architecture minimizes data collection by design.

2. Information We Process

• Account Authentication: Google Sign-In provides your basic profile (UID, email address, display name) secured by Google Firebase Auth.
• Calendar Events: Events you extract or create are stored locally on your device (AsyncStorage) and synchronized to your private Firestore account (/users/{uid}/events).
• Campus Affiliation: Your chosen college or campus tenant to deliver campus-wide event broadcasts.
• Push Tokens: Expo Push Tokens for high-priority campus notices.

3. AI Event Extraction Privacy

When you share or paste unstructured text into Vanko, text is transmitted via TLS 1.3 encrypted HTTPS to our secure cloud backend. Only the date, title, time, and link fields are extracted. Raw shared message contents are not permanently stored on disk or used to train third-party AI models.

4. Data Deletion & Your Rights

Under the Indian DPDP Act 2023 and Google Play Store policies, you retain the complete right to data erasure at any time. You can delete your account and all associated events instantly from the app settings or via our public Account Deletion Portal.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or data privacy inquiries, contact our team at supportvanko@gmail.com.
